Simple 4-Step Process

We've streamlined our admission process to make it as smooth and welcoming as possible for your family.

Initial Inquiry

Submit an online inquiry form or visit our school to learn more about our programs and facilities.

Application Submission

Complete the formal application form and provide necessary documents including birth certificates and previous transcripts.

Assessment & Interview

Prospective students participate in a friendly placement assessment, followed by a family interview with our heads of department.

Enrollment & Orientation

Upon acceptance, secure the spot by paying the enrollment fee and attending our comprehensive new student orientation.
