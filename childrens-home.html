Child Protection Standards

Our Zero-Tolerance Safeguarding Policy

At Tumaini Children's Home, the safety, dignity, and mental well-being of every child is our utmost priority. We strictly comply with the Kenya Directorate of Children's Services regulations and global safeguarding frameworks.

Strict Staff Vetting: Comprehensive police clearance certificates and mandatory child-protection background checks for all workers and volunteers.

Secure Gated Facility: 24-hour perimeter security, biometric visitor logging, and strict parental/guardian verification protocol.

Individual Care Plans: Customized psycho-social support, academic tracking, and case management for every resident child.

Whistleblowing & Advocacy: Direct reporting mechanisms and unannounced oversight visits by certified child welfare officers.

Admissions & Placement

How Children Join Our Residential Family

Placements at Tumaini Children's Home are conducted in strict partnership with the Kenya Directorate of Children's Services, local administrative chiefs, and certified social workers to ensure vulnerable children receive urgent sanctuary.

1

Referral & Reporting

Cases are referred by children's officers, community elders, or legal guardians detailing the child's vulnerability status.

2

Social Assessment

Our resident social work team conducts a home visit, trauma assessment, and medical clearance to formulate an individual care plan.

3

Warm Integration

The child is welcomed into a family dormitory unit, enrolled in school, and assigned a caring house parent and peer mentor.
