HOLISTIC DEVELOPMENT

Co-Curricular Pathways for Every Learner

Through Kenya’s Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC), learners cultivate their innate talents, discover passions, and build resilient character.

Sports & Athletics

Championship football pitches, athletics tracks, volleyball, and guided swimming lessons promoting physical stamina, team camaraderie, and sportsmanship.

Football • Volleyball • Track • Swimming

STEM & Discovery

Hands-on science lab experiments, basic coding, mathematics clubs, and regional science fairs encouraging critical thinking and practical problem solving.

Science Fair • Coding • Math Club

Creative & Performing Arts

Vibrant music bands, school choir, drama and poetry recitations, and visual canvas painting enabling pupils to express creativity with pride.

Music • Drama • Fine Arts • Choir

Leadership & Character

Active Student Council, Scouting & Girl Guides movements, debating society, and environmental conservation projects shaping responsible future leaders.

Student Council • Scouts • Debate Club

WHY LEARNERS THRIVE

An Enriching, Supportive Campus Culture

Every day at Tumaini Comprehensive School is intentionally structured to maximize academic curiosity, individual well-being, and social camaraderie.

Spacious & Secure 10-Acre Campus

A secure, gated environment surrounded by lush green lawns and fresh air conducive for intense study and healthy outdoor play.

Balanced Nutrition & Wellness

Fresh farm-to-plate hot lunches and snacks prepared daily to keep learners energized and focused throughout the school day.

Remedial Support & Mentorship

Personalized tutoring sessions for learners requiring extra academic reinforcement to ensure no student is left behind.

DAILY ROUTINE

A Typical Day at Tumaini

Balancing core academics with wellness, practical CBC activities, and co-curricular passion.

07:30 AM – 08:15 AM

Arrival & Character Assembly

Students arrive safely, participate in morning devotion/assembly, national anthem, and moral values briefing.

08:15 AM – 12:45 PM

Core CBC Learning Blocks

In-depth academic lessons in Sciences, Mathematics, Languages, Social Studies, and Practical ICT.

12:45 PM – 01:45 PM

Hot Lunch & Free Recreation

Nutritious communal dining hall meal followed by relaxed playground socialization and supervised rest.

01:45 PM – 04:30 PM

Clubs, Sports & Departure

Co-curricular clubs, athletics coaching, remedial study support, followed by safe departure or bus pickup.
